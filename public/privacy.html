Lawful bases and data protection rights

Under UK data protection law, we must have a "lawful basis" for collecting and using your personal information. There is a list of possible lawful bases in the UK GDPR. You can find out more about lawful bases on the ICO's website.

Which lawful basis we rely on may affect your data protection rights which are set out in brief below. You can find out more about your data protection rights and the exemptions which may apply on the ICO's website:

  • Your right of access — You have the right to ask us for copies of your personal information. You can request other information such as details about where we get personal information from and who we share personal information with. There are some exemptions which means you may not receive all the information you ask for.
  • Your right to rectification — You have the right to ask us to correct or delete personal information you think is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Your right to erasure — You have the right to ask us to delete your personal information.
  • Your right to restriction of processing — You have the right to ask us to limit how we can use your personal information.
  • Your right to object to processing — You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data.
  • Your right to data portability — You have the right to ask that we transfer the personal information you gave us to another organisation, or to you.
  • Your right to withdraw consent — When we use consent as our lawful basis you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

If you make a request, we must respond to you without undue delay and in any event within one month. To make a data protection rights request, please contact us using the contact details at the top of this privacy notice.

Service improvement requires understanding usage patterns to identify friction points and improve features. How long we keep information

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which we collected it, and in accordance with UK GDPR and applicable UK law. In summary:

  • Your account and profile data: kept while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of you closing your account, or after 24 months of continuous inactivity.
  • CVs and uploaded documents: deleted within 30 days of account closure.
  • Job listings, applications and bookings: up to 12 months for listings and applications; up to 6 years for confirmed bookings, for record-keeping and dispute resolution under the Limitation Act 1980.
  • Messages between users: deleted when either party deletes their account.
  • Financial and transaction records: 6 years, to comply with the Companies Act 2006 and HMRC requirements.
  • Marketing consent and unsubscribe records: consent records kept for 3 years after withdrawal; unsubscribe records kept indefinitely to prevent accidental re-contact, as required by PECR.
  • Backups: rolling 30-day period. Data deleted at your request may persist in backups for a further 30 days before being fully purged.
  • Queries, complaints and claims: 6 years from resolution, in line with the Limitation Act 1980.

Where a specific retention period is required by UK law (for example, HMRC tax records or right-to-work check records), we retain data for the period the law requires and then securely delete or anonymise it.

We maintain a detailed internal Data Retention Schedule setting out retention periods for each category of data we process. You can request a copy by contacting us at the email address above.

You may request earlier deletion of your personal data at any time under your right to erasure (UK GDPR Article 17), subject to any overriding legal or contractual retention requirements.
